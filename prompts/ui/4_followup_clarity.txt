1) EXPLAIN THE SCREEN

     "Things I found in your bank statements and emails that may need you."
   - At the end of that line, a small ⓘ icon. Tapping it opens a small bottom sheet "How FinAssist works" with:
     • 🏦 Bank statements and ✉️ emails are checked for things that affect your money.
