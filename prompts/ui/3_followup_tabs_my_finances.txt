Update the app as below. Keep the current look (colours, fonts, illustrations, slim cards), data access, the detail bottom sheet and the feedback buttons as they are.

=== A) TABS ===
- Delete the "Upcoming" tab, its page and its code.
- Rename the "Recurring" tab to "My Finances" (icon: wallet).
- The bottom bar has two tabs, evenly spaced: "Attention" and "My Finances". Keep the tab bar as a list so a tab can be added later without redesign.

=== B) ATTENTION TAB ===
1) Briefing card at the top: COUNTS ONLY, always exactly 2 short lines, fixed height (never grows), with the existing illustration on the right.
   Use the same groups as the cards below (Overdue, This week, Coming up, Good to know) and count the CARDS shown (a stacked card counts as 1):
   - If Overdue > 0:   line 1 "{o} overdue · {w} this week" (soft red tint),        line 2 "{rest} can wait"
   - Else if This week > 0: line 1 "{w} need you this week" (soft amber tint),     line 2 "{rest} can wait"
   - Else if any cards:  line 1 "Nothing urgent this week 🌿" (soft mint tint),    line 2 "{rest} can wait"
   - No cards at all:    line 1 "All clear 🌿" (soft mint tint), hide line 2.
   ({rest} = Coming up + Good to know. Use singular/plural correctly: "1 needs you", "3 need you". Hide "· 0 this week".)
   Never write fixed numbers or names in this card – everything is calculated from the data.

2) Stack cards with the same payee:
   - Cards of the same account whose normalised_payee matches (case-insensitive, trimmed) are shown as ONE card: "{Payee} · {n} updates".
   - The stack goes into the group of its MOST URGENT item (Overdue > This week > Coming up > Good to know); its date pill shows the nearest date.
   - The stack's text line shows the most urgent item's headline/suggestion (2 lines max); the amount shows that item's amount.
   - Tapping a stack opens a bottom sheet listing its items (each as a slim row: type label, date pill, amount, 1-line text); tapping an item opens its normal detail sheet.
   - A payee with only one card stays a normal card.

=== C) MY FINANCES TAB (was Recurring) ===
1) Top card "Your month at a glance" (replaces the plain teal total card), same style as the Attention briefing, fixed height, illustration on the right (calm house/calendar scene in the teal palette):
   - Line 1: "Regular payments: {Y} € / month"  (Y = sum of recurring_payment amounts)
   - Line 2 (only if a regular_income signal exists): "In {X} € · Left {X−Y} €" (Left in green if positive, red if negative)
   - Line 3 (only if > 0): "{n} heads-up(s) below" = number of payment rows that carry a tag (see 4). Soft amber tint if n > 0, otherwise soft mint.
2) Donut "Where it goes": keep it. In the centre show the largest category and its share (e.g. "Rent 77%"). Top 3 categories next to it with amount and %.
3) Payment list: one SLIM ROW per payment (about 60px), grouped by category with a small header "● Category · total".
   Row: category icon in a pastel circle (category icons only, no brand logos) · payee (1 line, truncate with …) · monthly amount. Do not repeat the category name inside the row.
4) Tags that link to what is coming (generic, from data – never hard-code names):
   For each recurring payment, look for an Attention signal of the same account whose normalised_payee matches (case-insensitive, trimmed), or whose details.subscriptions list contains that payee:
   - price_change / price_increase with a date today or later: amber tag "→ {new amount} € from {date}".
   - price_change / price_increase with a past date or no date: amber tag "Price went up".
   - duplicate_subscription: purple tag "Possible duplicate".
   - any other Attention signal: grey tag "Needs a look".
   At most one tag per row (priority in the order above). Tapping the tag opens that signal's detail sheet.
5) Income section at the bottom: slim rows (payee, amount in green). Hide the section if there is no income.

=== D) GENERAL RULES FOR ALL SCREENS (robust when cards are added or removed) ===
- Never hard-code payees, amounts, counts, dates or category names; everything comes from the data.
- Every list must look right with 0, 1 or many items: 0 -> friendly empty state with a small illustration; many -> the list scrolls while headers, top cards, charts and the bottom bar keep their size.
- Top cards have a fixed height; they never grow with the data.
- Hide empty groups and sections instead of showing blank boxes.
- Long text: payee names 1 line with …, descriptions 2 lines max. Amounts never wrap or overflow.
- Missing values (null amount, no date, unknown category) are left out; unknown categories use the grey "other" icon. Never show "null", "undefined", "NaN", or "0,00 €" for a missing amount.
- Charts: one category -> full ring; more than 6 categories -> top 5 plus "Other".
- Counts and badges are always calculated from the current data.
- Switching person reloads all data; nothing from the previous person stays on screen.
